MEADOW — FARM DECISIONS: PHONE INSTRUCTIONS

1. Tap the game link your tutor posts in Teams. If it opens in a Teams or ChatGPT preview, use the menu to open it in your phone’s usual browser.
2. Read the short guide and tap Play / return to farm. No app installation, payment or ChatGPT login is needed.
3. Keep Autopilot off. Use Buy / upgrade to find purchases and My figures to check income, costs, cash and unpaid bills.
4. Review a purchase before buying. Compare its cost, extra net profit, payback time and cash left. You may choose Save for later. Explain your reason.
5. Follow the next-stage checklist to develop your farm. Different enterprises are needed to advance.
6. Respond to random events. Read the descriptions and effects, compare the costs and profit, then choose a response. Record a reason and reflection. The game pauses while you decide.
7. At the end, tap My report. Complete the reflections and download the report. Save it to your phone and upload or attach it to the Teams assignment as your tutor asks.

Continue on the same phone in the same browser. Progress is stored locally, not through your Teams account. Private browsing, clearing browser data or changing devices can lose your saved farm. Download a learning report at the end of every session.
You can pause the game. It also pauses when its tab is hidden or closed. Avoid Restart unless you intend to clear your farm.

Aim to justify good decisions about costs, cash flow and welfare, not simply earn the most money. All money and forecasts are simplified teaching values.
